Independent by design

This isn't affiliated with BlackBox, doesn't mirror their private interface or read your account cookies, and never stores your FTP credentials in the portable HTML. BlackBox can update its website without preventing the local workspace from opening.

The FTP directory and the CSV schema remain configurable because BlackBox may change them. There is no verified public metadata-submission API in this build, so the CSV import and final submission occur in the official portal.
